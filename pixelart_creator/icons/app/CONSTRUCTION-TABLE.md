# Application icon family - construction table

Generated by `scripts/derive_app_icons.py` from its own `FAMILY` data and
from the committed masters' measured pixels - do not hand-edit; regenerate
with the deriver.

Every member is a committed master of the same size (k = 1) or of a
whole-divisor size scaled by an integer factor k with nearest-neighbour
sampling only. Nothing is cropped, resampled or redrawn by a tool.

## Container assembly

- `pixelart-creator.ico` (Windows): members 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256 px, assembled explicitly - no size is left for a library's own resize fallback.
- `pixelart-creator.icns` (macOS): 11 entries, each a PNG of a family member at exactly its pixel size, written by this script:
  - `icp4`: 16x16 px (`app-icon-16.png`)
  - `icp5`: 32x32 px (`app-icon-32.png`)
  - `icp6`: 64x64 px (`app-icon-64.png`)
  - `ic07`: 128x128 px (`app-icon-128.png`)
  - `ic08`: 256x256 px (`app-icon-256.png`)
  - `ic09`: 512x512 px (`app-icon-512.png`)
  - `ic10`: 1024x1024 px (`app-icon-1024.png`)
  - `ic11`: 32x32 px (`app-icon-32.png`)
  - `ic12`: 64x64 px (`app-icon-64.png`)
  - `ic13`: 256x256 px (`app-icon-256.png`)
  - `ic14`: 512x512 px (`app-icon-512.png`)
