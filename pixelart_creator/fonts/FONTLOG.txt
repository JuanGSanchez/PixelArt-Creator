Basic Font Information
----------------------

Nifen Art is an original pixel typeface for user interfaces: menus, titles,
tabs and buttons. It comes in two files, Regular and Bold, which form one
style-linked family named "Nifen Art".

Design facts:

- One em is 12 pixels. Every pixel is a square of 128 font units, so the em is
  1536 units. The glyphs are drawn on that grid and are meant to be shown at
  12 px, or at whole multiples of 12 px, without antialiasing.
- Latin capitals are 7 pixels tall, lowercase letters 5, ascenders 8 and
  descenders 2. Kana and other full-width glyphs sit in a 12 pixel cell.
- Regular strokes are 1 pixel wide, Bold strokes 2 pixels. Bold is Regular
  shifted one pixel to the right and merged with it (advance one pixel wider),
  corrected by hand-drawn glyphs wherever that rule would close a counter or
  fuse two parts, and for a few frequent letters (r n m R and the Cyrillic
  Ya) wherever it would close the gap between a stem and its arm or leg.
- Latin, Greek and Cyrillic letters have proportional advances; the line box is
  11 pixels above the baseline and 3 below.

Character coverage (as of version 0.100, 771 characters in each weight):

- Basic Latin, Latin-1 Supplement and the whole of Latin Extended-A
  (U+0100-U+017F), the dotless j, and the capital sharp s (U+1E9E);
- Greek, modern monotonic set: capitals and lowercase letters, final sigma,
  tonos and dialytika forms, the Greek question mark, ano teleia, the numeral
  sign and the lower numeral sign (U+0370-U+03FF subset);
- Cyrillic, the whole range U+0400-U+045F (Russian, Ukrainian, Belarusian,
  Serbian, Macedonian), the letters Ghe with upturn (U+0490, U+0491) and the
  numero sign (U+2116);
- punctuation and typographic quotation marks, the thin space and the narrow
  no-break space used by French typesetting, arrows, mathematical and
  geometric symbols and a few currency signs;
- hiragana (U+3041-U+3096, U+309D-U+309F) and katakana (U+30A1-U+30FA,
  U+30FC-U+30FF), complete, with the small kana, the voiced and semi-voiced
  forms, the prolonged sound mark and the middle dot; the spacing voiced
  and semi-voiced marks (U+309B, U+309C);
- Japanese punctuation: the ideographic space, the comma and full stop,
  the ditto and iteration marks, the brackets U+3008-U+3011 and the wave
  dash, and the full-width forms used in interface text (! ( ) , . : ; ? ~).

Not included: ideographs (kanji), the combining voiced marks U+3099 and U+309A,
combining marks (U+0300-U+036F), polytonic Greek, Latin Extended-B beyond the
dotless j, other Cyrillic extensions.

Design notes:

- Every glyph is an original drawing made for this grid. No glyph, outline or
  metric was copied or adapted from another font.
- Where two letters have the same shape on this grid (for example Latin A,
  Greek Alpha and Cyrillic A) they share one drawing; letters that differ at
  all, even slightly, are drawn separately.
- Kana fill a 10 by 11 pixel box inside the 12 pixel cell. The hiragana were
  redrawn with one rule in mind: letters that are easy to confuse at 12 pixels
  must differ by a feature that survives in both weights (for example nu has a
  small closing loop at the end of its last stroke where me has none; ha and
  ho differ by the second bar that crosses the right-hand stem of ho; sa has
  one bar and a stem that runs on into its hook, ki two bars and a hook of its
  own). Curves are approximated by clean diagonals of 1:1 and 1:2.
- Small hiragana are about three quarters of the full height (at most 8 of 11
  rows), centred in the cell and set on its bottom row, as in horizontal
  Japanese text. The small katakana keep their earlier placement at the left
  of the cell.
- Voiced and semi-voiced kana are the plain kana plus the voicing mark at the
  upper right, composed by rule: the part of the base that would touch the mark
  is cut away, so the mark never touches the base in either weight. Where that
  cut would remove a feature that tells the letter apart, the glyph is drawn
  by hand.
- The fonts are generated from plain-text glyph grids by a deterministic build:
  rebuilding the same sources gives byte-identical files.

Reserved Font Name: "Nifen Art". The author declared this on 10 October 2026,
and the declaration is made in the copyright line of OFL.txt and in the
copyright record (name ID 0) of both font files. Under clause 3 of the SIL Open
Font License a Modified Version of this Font Software may not use the name
"Nifen Art" as its primary font name unless the copyright holder gives written
permission; a modified font needs another name. The rest of the licence is
unchanged.

ChangeLog
---------

10 October 2026 (JuanGSanchez)
  Nifen Art version 0.100, work in progress.
  - Basic Latin, Latin-1 Supplement and Latin Extended-A, Regular and Bold.
  - User interface symbols: arrows, geometric shapes, mathematical signs.
  - Greek (modern monotonic) and Cyrillic (U+0400-U+045F, U+0490, U+0491,
    U+2116), Regular and Bold.
  - French and German typography: oe ligatures, sharp s and capital sharp s,
    guillemets, low and high quotation marks, thin space and narrow no-break
    space.
  - Double acute drawn as two separate strokes; the comma below on K, k, N, n,
    R and r drawn detached from the letter.
  - Hiragana and katakana, complete (including small kana, voiced and
    semi-voiced forms), Japanese punctuation and full-width forms.
  - Hiragana redrawn (the 46 basic letters, wi, we, the small forms, the
    iteration marks and the yori ligature), Regular and Bold, in the same
    12 pixel cell: confusable letters differ by a feature that survives at
    12 pixels; small hiragana centred in the cell; the voicing marks redesigned
    around cuts that keep the letter recognisable. Katakana, Latin, Greek and
    Cyrillic are unchanged.
  - Reserved Font Name "Nifen Art" declared (OFL.txt and the copyright record
    of both fonts).
  - Bold correction pass: hand-drawn Bold forms that keep the gap between
    strokes for r, n, m, R, Greek eta and pi, eng, the tilde, the small tilde
    and almost-equal signs, Cyrillic Ya (both cases) and Lje (both cases).
    The Regular weight is unchanged.
