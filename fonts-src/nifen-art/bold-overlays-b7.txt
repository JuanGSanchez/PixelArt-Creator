# Hand-drawn Bold grids, correction pass for gap closure. The derived Bold
# (Regular OR Regular shifted one pixel right) closes every one-pixel gap
# between two strokes; the glyphs here keep the gap that makes the letter.
# Rule used for every grid: the same box as the derived Bold (the Bold advance
# is Regular + 1, so no stroke may leave it), every stroke at least two pixels
# wide, and the counters and parts of the Regular glyph kept.
