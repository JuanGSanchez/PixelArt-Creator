# Bold correction drawings for the hiragana adapted from Ark Pixel Font 12 px (monospaced,
# Japanese variant, version 2026.09.25; https://github.com/TakWolf/ark-pixel-font;
# Copyright (c) 2021, TakWolf (https://takwolf.com); SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1,
# no Reserved Font Name). The Bold of every hiragana is derived from the Regular drawing by the
# rule of this typeface (Regular merged with Regular shifted one pixel to the right). Where
# that rule closes a counter or fuses two parts of a letter, the record below replaces it with
# a drawing that is the derived Bold with the fewest changes that restore the counters and
# the parts, with every stroke at least two pixels wide. The voicing marks are drawn as two
# blocks with a one-pixel gap (voiced) or a five-pixel-wide diamond with a one-pixel counter
# (semi-voiced). Three records (U+3056, U+305A, U+3062) are drawn by hand: there the derived Bold
# keeps every part and counter but fuses the two voicing ticks into one block.
