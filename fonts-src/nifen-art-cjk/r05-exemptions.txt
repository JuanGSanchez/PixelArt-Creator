# R05 exemptions of the CJK face: Regular glyphs that may hold a 2x2 pixel block.
#
# One line per glyph:
#   U+XXXX set=<jp|sc|kr> reason=<text>
# reason= is the last item of the line and runs to the end of it. An entry with no reason
# is not a declaration: the lint refuses it and names it. An undeclared block fails the
# lint and names the glyph. An entry for a glyph that has no block, an entry that names no
# glyph of the set, and a glyph declared twice (here and by the solid flag of its record)
# are refused too. The lint prints the number of exempted glyphs and the name of each.
# A glyph flagged solid in its own record is exempt in the same way and is counted with
# these; it keeps its reason in that record, not here.
#
# Three kinds of syllable hold a 2x2 block, and each is named by its reason:
#   - a syllable whose final consonant is the aspirated t: three bars (top, middle, bottom)
#     cannot keep a pixel apart in the four-row final slot, so the middle bar sits on the
#     bottom bar;
#   - a syllable whose initial consonant is the aspirated t, beside a flat vowel and a final
#     consonant: the initial has only three rows, so its middle bar sits on the top and the
#     bottom bar;
#   - a syllable whose initial consonant is h under the same conditions: the bar and the foot
#     of the ring share columns in the three rows.
# Every other drawn syllable keeps one-pixel strokes. The user set no maximum; the lint prints
# the count.
