# measured set: exactly the Han characters the shipped text uses, each in the form of the
# Chinese standard (the table of general standard Chinese characters). A character

# are the pilot's.
#
# The record forms are the ones described in han-jp.txt, with set=sc. A record may be the
# drawing of this set only: same-as= is refused, and a drawing is never shared with the other form set, even where both standards
# give the character the same form.
