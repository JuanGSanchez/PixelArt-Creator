# Citation records of the Han drawings of the Nifen Art CJK face.
#
# One record per line, fields separated by " | ", UTF-8, LF. A record says which
# standard, and which place in it, a drawing is made for. The standards decide which
# form a drawing shows; no drawing is taken from any font, and no step checks that a
# drawing shows the form printed at its cited place. The record lets a reader audit it.
#
#   JP | U+65E5 | joyo-2010     | page=<n> pos=<entry No. in the Joyo list> | via=<id>
#   JP | U+xxxx | hyogai-2000   | page=<n> slot=<No. in the table of forms>  | via=<id>
#   SC | U+65E5 | tongyong-2013 | page=<n> cell=<0001-8105>                 | via=<id>
#   JP | U+77E9 | no-entry      | absent=joyo-2010,hyogai-2000              | via=membership
#
# A Japanese-form drawing (JP) cites the Joyo kanji table (joyo-2010) or the table of
# forms for characters outside it (hyogai-2000). A Simplified-form drawing (SC) cites the
# table of general standard Chinese characters (tongyong-2013). A character of the
# Japanese-form set that is listed in neither Japanese table has no standard entry: its
# Japanese-form drawing carries the no-entry record, which names the two lists it is
# absent from and cites no place. The no-entry record is valid only for a member of the
# class recorded in no-entry-class.txt by the last acceptance run, and on the
# Japanese-form drawing alone.
#
# pos is the 1-based position of the character among the non-comment lines of the Joyo
# list. via names the instrument that established the place: textlayer (the page's text
# layer holds the character at that position), hyogai-confirmed, index-agree (an
# independent index of the table gives the same cell), membership, template-identity
# (the identity test of a slot of the table of forms against the recorded character) or
# identity-review (the two-reader identity check of a cell or slot).
#
# A record is required for each drawing of a character that is in both form sets, and for
# the Japanese-form drawing of each member of the no-entry class. A record of any other
# drawing is allowed and checked. A drawing whose record is empty, missing, malformed or
# of another standard counts among the drawings without a cited place, and the lint fails.
