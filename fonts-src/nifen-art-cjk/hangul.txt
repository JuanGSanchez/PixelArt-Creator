# Hangul set of the Nifen Art CJK face (family "Nifen Art CJK KR").
#
# One record per Hangul syllable (U+AC00-U+D7A3) of this set, and nothing else. The
# drawing list is the measured set: exactly the syllables the shipped text uses. The
# conjoining and compatibility jamo are not part of this set. A Hangul record has no
# same-as: the Han form sets do not share drawings with it.
#
# The record forms are the ones described in han-jp.txt, with set=kr:
#   glyph U+XXXX set=kr src=drawn top=<row> x=<column>      a syllable drawn whole
#   glyph U+XXXX set=kr src=recipe:<part>,<part>,...         a syllable composed of parts
#   glyph part.<name> set=kr src=drawn top=<row> x=<column>  a drawing that recipes use
# A recipe is the union of its parts, each already placed in the cell by its own top= and
# x=, so a recipe carries no offsets. Every part is a drawing made for this typeface.
#
# How a syllable is built. The cell is 11 x 11 pixels, strokes are one pixel wide, and a
# syllable has an initial consonant, a vowel and, when it has one, a final consonant. The
# vowels fall into three families and each family has its own layout:
#   A  vowels written upright, to the right of the initial (a ya eo yeo i, and ae e ye,
#      which have two upright strokes). Rows are counted from the top of the cell, 0..10.
#        no final:    initial 7 rows tall (rows 2..8), vowel the full height (rows 0..10)
#        with final:  initial 6 rows tall (rows 0..5), vowel rows 0..5, a blank row 6,
#                     final consonant rows 7..10
#      The initial is 7 columns wide beside a one-stroke vowel (columns 0..6; the vowel
#      stands in columns 8..10) and 6 columns wide beside a two-stroke vowel (columns
#      0..5; the vowel stands in columns 7..10).
#   B  vowels written flat, under the initial (o yo u yu eu)
#        no final:    initial 7 rows tall (rows 0..6, columns 2..8), a blank row, the vowel in
#                     rows 8..10 (the bar of eu in row 9); the bar is 11 columns wide
#        with final:  initial 3 rows tall (rows 0..2, columns 2..8), a blank row, the vowel in
#                     rows 4..5 (the bar of eu in row 4), a blank row 6, final rows 7..10
#   C  vowels that combine both (wa wae oe wo we wi ui): the flat part under the initial on
#      the left (7 columns wide, 6 beside a two-stroke upright vowel; the initial the same
#      width, in the rows of family B) and the upright part of family A on the right, so
#      wa = o + a, wae = o + ae, oe = o + i, wo = u + eo, we = u + e, wi = u + i, ui = eu + i.
# Part names tell the layout: part.iA<f><w>-<jamo> is an initial of family A (<f> is 1
# when a final follows, <w> is s for a one-stroke vowel and d for a two-stroke vowel),
# part.vA<f>-<vowel> a vowel, part.iB<f>-<jamo> and part.vB<f>-<vowel> the initial and the
# vowel of family B, part.iC<f><w>-<jamo> and part.fC<f><n>-<flat vowel> the initial and the flat
# part of family C (<n> its width), part.f-<jamo> a final consonant 7 columns wide (columns 2..8,
# rows 7..10) and part.fw-<jamo> a double final consonant 11 columns wide.
#
# Where a built syllable reads badly at this size, a syllable drawn whole replaces its
# recipe; the lint lists no override, and the record simply is a drawing.
#
# A Regular glyph may hold a 2x2 pixel block only if it is declared in
# r05-exemptions.txt, with a reason.
