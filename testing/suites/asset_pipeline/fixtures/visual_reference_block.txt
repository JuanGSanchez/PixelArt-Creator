Visual references of the redrawn Han drawings

Two regional builds of one typeface family were looked at as a visual
reference, one for each form set:

  Source Han Sans, Regular, Version 2.005;addfeatures 5.0.0b21: Japanese form
  Source Han Sans SC, Regular, Version 2.005;addfeatures 5.0.0b21: Simplified form

Owner: © 2014-2025 Adobe (http://www.adobe.com/)

  Japanese form: 394
  Simplified form: 534

They were used only as a visual reference, looked at by eye, for the
redrawn drawings. No glyph, outline, bitmap or other data was taken from
them or from any other font, and no part of them is in the font.
